About Radio Free Sheboygan

Brought to you from the very navel of the Universe, Sheboygan, Radio Free Sheboygan is an independent audio project documenting local politics, arts, and culture. We produce long-form interviews, short features, and a rotating photo series. Our mission: preserve local voice and question power. FM Sounds, AM Soul.

Clogwino (ossia, Pym)

Emerging from a rigorously informed dialogue with post-conceptualism, Clogwinos’s work operates within a self-reflexive framework that interrogates authorship, materiality, and the conditionality of convention as an institution in and of itself. Drawing on a lineage that spans late modernist formalism and contemporary critical theory, Clogwinos’s practice privileges process over product, foregrounding gestures that resist easy legibility while remaining deeply embedded in art-historical discourse. Through a calibrated tension between opacity and intention, Clogwinos’s work positions itself less as an object to be consumed than as a proposition to be encountered, inviting sustained engagement from viewers attuned to the nuanced vocabularies of the contemporary avant-garde.
